About me · Lepsza Strona Djembe

Making drums since 1997

The Lepsza Strona Djembe drum workshop was founded in 1997. Since then I have built several thousand drums from cultures around the world: djembe, dundun, ashiko, Cuban cajóns, ceremonial bass drums, natural-hide gongs and shaman drums.

Passion and decades of experience in instrument making have resulted in professional drums with a powerful, grounding sound.

    Air-tube tuning

    Damp air? Pump it up and keep playing.

    In the evening or in damp air a natural hide quickly loses its tuning. My drums have an air tube between the frame and the hide – a few strokes of the pump and the sound is back where you want it.

    ↑

    More air – higher, brighter sound.

    ↓

    Less air – deep, gong-like bass.

    ♪

    A very wide range of tones. Play deep bass with a beater or use finger technique.
